# HS-AeroTS-FL Paper Writing Rules

This directory contains the HS-AeroTS-FL manuscript targeting MDPI *Drones*.

## Core Principles

1. Before writing, prioritize the current paper outline, existing manuscript,
   verified results under `reports/`, and the project `README.md`.
2. Never invent experimental results, parameters, references, implementation
   details, or statistical conclusions. Mark uncertain information for verification.
3. Follow the current outline and edit only the requested section unless broader
   revision is explicitly requested.
4. Use concise, formal, and objective academic English suitable for *Drones*.
   Avoid repetition, generic filler, and exaggerated novelty claims.

## Scientific Boundaries

- Prefer the terms `runtime anomaly detection`, `fault-domain diagnosis`,
  `architecture-aware diagnostic evidence`, and `software-module suspect ranking`.
- Do not describe SHAP attribution or static PX4 uORB mapping as causal analysis
  or root-cause localization.
- Do not claim that Cascade consistently outperforms Direct Five-Class unless
  supported by experimental evidence.
- Clearly distinguish fixed chronological, purged, leave-log-out, ALFA, and PX4
  replay experiments.
- Preserve the P9 detector-gated negative result as an important capability boundary.

## Writing Practice

- Keep Results focused on experimental observations and quantitative evidence;
reserve broader interpretation for Discussion.

- Prefer targeted revision over unnecessary rewriting.

- Before finishing, check numerical accuracy, terminology consistency, and whether
all claims remain within the evidence supported by the experiments.

- After completing a paragraph, subsection, or section, briefly recommend the most logical next step, such as the next section to draft or another task that moves the manuscript toward completion.
