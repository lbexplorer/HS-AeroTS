\subsection{Data Splits and Statistical Protocol}

The fixed chronological split provides the primary reproduction baseline and preserves the per-log temporal order used by P2 and P3. The purged protocol removes 14 windows on each side of split boundaries to test sensitivity to temporal proximity. Leave-log-out assigns complete flight logs, rather than individual windows, to train, validation, and test partitions; it is the principal test of generalization to unseen logs. These protocols address different risks and are reported together rather than treated as interchangeable estimates.

The primary real-flight and purged experiments use five frozen model seeds. Thresholds are selected on validation data only. Seed standard deviations quantify variation among the five fitted models; they are not sampling intervals. Absolute Stage 1 AUPRC, Stage 2 Macro-F1, Cascade Macro-F1, and Direct Five-Class Macro-F1 are additionally assigned 95\% cluster-bootstrap intervals using 1000 resamples of complete test flight logs with seed 20260825. Each resample is evaluated for all five frozen model seeds and then averaged, so overlapping windows from a flight remain in the same cluster. ALFA intervals resample its 46 sequences and Uncategorized intervals resample its 1017 flights. Paired Cascade-versus-Direct and explanation-masking intervals use the same flight-level principle. Replay intervals use 5000 two-way cluster-bootstrap repetitions that independently resample the three source logs and four mutation types. This preserves the crossed dependence structure, although inference remains low-resolution because each cluster dimension is small. Protocol-specific counts, parameters, software versions, and frozen statistical settings are listed in Table~\ref{tab:protocol} and Appendix~\ref{app:reproducibility}.
