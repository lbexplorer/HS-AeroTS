\subsection{Data Splits and Statistical Protocol}

The fixed chronological split provides the primary reproduction baseline and preserves the per-log temporal order used by P2 and P3. The purged protocol removes 14 windows on each side of split boundaries to test sensitivity to temporal proximity. Leave-log-out assigns complete flight logs, rather than individual windows, to train, validation, and test partitions; it is the principal test of generalization to unseen logs. These protocols address different risks and are reported together rather than treated as interchangeable estimates.

The primary real-flight and purged experiments use five seeds. Thresholds are selected on validation data only. Seed standard deviations quantify model-training variation, not sampling uncertainty. Paired Cascade-versus-Direct and explanation-masking differences are estimated with 1000 flight-log-level bootstrap repetitions at 95\% confidence; flight logs, not overlapping windows, are the sampling unit. Replay intervals use 5000 two-way cluster-bootstrap repetitions that independently resample the three source logs and four mutation types. This preserves the crossed dependence structure, although inference remains low-resolution because each cluster dimension is small. Frozen settings and per-run records are available for confidential editorial or reviewer assessment as described in the Data Availability Statement.
