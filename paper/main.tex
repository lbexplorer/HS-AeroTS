\Title{Hierarchical Runtime Anomaly Diagnosis from Real PX4 Telemetry with Commit-Level Architecture-Aware Inspection Evidence}

\addhighlights{enabled}
\renewcommand{\addhighlights}{
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
    \item A two-stage hierarchy separates real-flight anomaly detection from four-domain diagnosis.
    \item TreeSHAP evidence is conserved across features, channels, uORB topics, and subsystems.
    \item Commit-matched, Stage-1-gated analysis exposes detector losses before module inspection.
    \item Strict isolation and controlled replay expose leakage sensitivity and detector-gated limits.
\end{itemize}}

\abstract{Runtime anomaly detection identifies abnormal UAV telemetry but does not directly provide fault-domain or software inspection evidence. HS-AeroTS combines a LightGBM detector, a conditional four-domain classifier, hierarchical TreeSHAP aggregation, and a static PX4 uORB source-tree graph. On 1,389 UAV-SEAD logs, five-seed fixed-chronological AUPRC was \texorpdfstring{$0.7522 \pm 0.0039$}{0.7522 +/- 0.0039} (95\% flight-log CI [0.6915, 0.8080]); anomaly-only fault-domain Macro-F1 was \texorpdfstring{$0.8980 \pm 0.0020$}{0.8980 +/- 0.0020} [0.8581, 0.9296], and complete Cascade Macro-F1 was \texorpdfstring{$0.6902 \pm 0.0071$}{0.6902 +/- 0.0071} [0.6308, 0.7350]. Under leave-log-out isolation, detector AUPRC decreased to \texorpdfstring{$0.6296 \pm 0.0023$}{0.6296 +/- 0.0023} [0.4945, 0.7456]. Hierarchical aggregation conserved evidence mass, and attribution-guided masking caused greater degradation than random masking, but a document-derived semantic-map sensitivity showed near-random External Position and below-random Global Position Consistency@1. The ground-truth-anomaly-conditioned architecture analysis contained 1,393 matching-commit anomalous windows. Applying the frozen Stage 1 gate retained an average of 937.2 of these windows, missed 455.8, and introduced 532.2 matching-commit false-positive windows. Conservative source-path exclusion reduced the graph from 180 edges and 54 modules to 145 edges and 36 modules while retaining all 18 topics; this is a source-tree sensitivity, not build-target validation. In controlled replay, onset-conditioned ranking produced nonzero module recall with wide confidence intervals, but the unchanged detector identified 0 of 12 mutation runs. HS-AeroTS therefore provides conditional, traceable evidence for engineering inspection rather than causal or deployment-validated root-cause localization.}
\keyword{UAV telemetry; runtime anomaly detection; fault-domain diagnosis; explainable machine learning; PX4; uORB; source-tree inspection candidates}
